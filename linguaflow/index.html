LinguaFlow · Android

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the LinguaFlow mobile application handles information when you use it.

Effective: August 11, 2026 Package: uk.albezd.linguaflow.app

Summary

LinguaFlow does not require an account and the developer does not operate a server that receives your books, reading history, bookmarks, comments, or app settings. These items stay on your device. The Google Play version uses Google AdMob to display ads, and Google automatically receives certain device, usage, diagnostic, and network information as described below.

1. Scope and developer

This policy applies to the LinguaFlow Android application (the “App”), package name uk.albezd.linguaflow.app, provided by the LinguaFlow developer, al-bezd (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

2. Information stored locally

The App stores the following information locally on your device so that its reading features work and remain available offline:

  • books and book files that you import, including book text and metadata;
  • cover images that you select;
  • bookmarks and personal comments attached to bookmarks;
  • reading and display settings, app language, theme, hidden-book choices, and other preferences.

This information is stored using the App's local browser storage, including IndexedDB and local storage. We do not receive or remotely store it. The App reads only files you deliberately select through the file picker or provide by drag and drop.

3. Advertising and data handled by Google

The Google Play version of LinguaFlow includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and may show banner or interstitial advertisements. When the SDK is active, Google states that it automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location;
  • user interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views;
  • diagnostic information about the performance of the App and advertising SDK;
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and other applicable device identifiers.

Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Depending on your device settings, region, and consent choices, Google may serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your device's privacy settings.

For details, see Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Google Fonts

The App may request font files from Google Fonts. As with a normal web request, Google may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, device or browser information, and the date and time of the request. Google handles that information under its Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing you initiate

LinguaFlow lets you share a book or a bookmark through Android's system share interface. A shared bookmark may include the selected passage, its translation, and your comment. A shared book does not include your personal bookmarks or comments. Nothing is shared unless you choose the share action and select a destination.

Once you send content to another application or service, that recipient processes it under its own privacy practices. You should review the content before sharing it.

6. Permissions and accounts

The App requests Internet access to load ads and online resources. It does not request access to precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone. LinguaFlow does not provide account creation or sign-in.

7. Retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete the relevant book or bookmark, clear the App's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the App. Because we do not receive this local information, we cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

Information processed by Google is retained according to Google's own retention practices and controls.

8. Security

We limit the App's access to what is needed for its features and keep reading content locally. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Protect your device and avoid importing or sharing files containing information you do not want others to access.

9. Children

LinguaFlow is a general-audience reading tool and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through our own servers. Advertising services may apply age- and region-dependent protections. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way covered by this policy, contact us.

10. Your choices and rights

You control locally stored content through the App and Android settings. You can choose not to import or share content and can manage the advertising ID and applicable advertising privacy choices in your device settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have legal rights concerning personal data processed by third parties; requests about Google's processing should be directed to Google through its privacy controls.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date above. Material changes will be communicated in the App or store listing when appropriate.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning LinguaFlow, contact the developer at bezdenezhnykh.aleksandr@gmail.com.
